01 The brief

Make it modern. Lose nothing.

ASNK Trading Limited is the country representative in Ghana for four manufacturers: Church & Dwight (UK) for Arm & Hammer, Sarantis (Greece) for Fino, CeDo (Poland) for Paclan, and the Greek Canning Company for Kyknos tomatoes. Its website is where retailers and households go to see what ASNK carries and how to reach it.

The brief had two halves, and they pull against each other: make the site feel extremely modern, interactive and easy to navigate — and keep all of the existing content. No product dropped, no description rewritten away, no contact detail lost.

02 What we found

The content was right. The container wasn't.

We started by crawling the whole site — 59 pages across products, brands and categories — and measuring the Arm & Hammer page in a real browser. The problems fell into three groups.

Speed
Heavy for a catalogue

One brand page made 92 requests, loaded 43 scripts and 23 stylesheets, and moved about 1.7 MB — mostly page-builder and slider code, not products. On mobile data that's a real cost.

Finding things
No way to search

34 products sat behind brand pages, "Load More" buttons and "Read more" links. There was no search, no filtering, and Kyknos and OxiClean had no proper brand home.

First impression
A cookie wall and leftovers

Visitors met a full-screen cookie prompt before any product. Template leftovers ("Home 1", an "Uncategorized" category) and 12 of 15 images without alt text rounded it out.

A note on method

Figures come from a single measurement of asnktrading.com/arm-and-hammer in September 2026. Load times vary with connection, so we compare requests and page weight, which don't.

03 What we changed

A storefront, not a brochure.

Before
  • WordPress page builder, slider plugin and shop plugin to show a catalogue with no prices or cart
  • Browse brand by brand, click "Load More", then "Read more"
  • Cookie consent wall before the first product
  • Kyknos and OxiClean folded into other pages
  • Contact form behind a single page, phone number as plain text
After
  • A lightweight static site: no plugins to update, nothing to patch, no cookies to consent to
  • Instant search from any page (press /), plus filters by brand and category that live in the URL
  • Every product has its own page with the full description, related products and one-tap Call or Email enquiry
  • Five brand worlds, each in its own colours, with the manufacturer named
  • Tap-to-call, tap-to-email and a map to Ritz Junction on every contact surface

  1. Built for the phone first

    Most visitors in Ghana arrive on a mid-range Android phone. Layouts, tap targets and image sizes were designed at 360px first and scaled up.

  2. Interactive where it helps

    A floating product hero, a brand marquee, hover-tilt brand cards and quick-view drawers make the catalogue feel alive — and all of it calms down for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

  3. Enquiry is the conversion

    ASNK doesn't sell online, so "add to cart" was never the goal. Every product ends in a pre-filled call or email, so a retailer can ask about stock in one tap.

  4. Nothing to maintain

    Products live in one data file. Adding a product is one entry and a photo — no plugins, no theme updates, no database.

04 Five brand worlds

One distributor, five personalities.

The old site dressed every brand in the same template. The new one gives each brand its own colour world, built from its packaging, while the ASNK navy, red and gold hold the whole thing together.

07 What we learned

  1. A redesign is mostly a migration

    The riskiest part wasn't the visuals — it was making sure 34 descriptions, two inboxes and a P.O. Box survived. We crawled first and designed second.

  2. Match the tool to the job

    A shop plugin, a page builder and a slider plugin were running a catalogue with no checkout. A static site does that job faster, with nothing to patch.

  3. Design for how people actually enquire

    In this market, a call or an email is the sale. Putting that one tap away on every product mattered more than any animation.
